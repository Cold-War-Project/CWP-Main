# Democratic, constitutional, and reform character expansion

The displayed names follow the [alternate-timeline naming guide](naming.md). Historical people and organizations below document the research basis; their existence is not implied by an in-game label.

Research checked 26 September 2026. Each character ideology is a focused agenda with exactly four active law groups, rather than an attempt to make a complete biography. Other policies remain the concern of the interest group, other characters, and country institutions. Numerical preference strength, eligible constituencies, and dates are gameplay interpretations.

## Selection and integration

All twelve use the installed vanilla character-ideology schema with `character_ideology = yes`, matching interest-group eligibility in leader and nonleader scopes. Leader selection starts at weight 10, gains 5 for the principal constituency (the first metadata interest group), and gains 10 in the relevant institutional context. Nonleader selection starts at 10 and gains 5 for the principal constituency. These are relative weights, not percentages. Institutional context may mean a compatible policy already exists, or that a reformer faces the institution they oppose.

Country eligibility uses an era gate; Liberation Theologian additionally requires the existing Christian religious-heritage trait. No country tags force a historical personality or outcome. Dates represent the formation or wider political prominence of each selected current; older precursors are not denied. The Presidential Developmentalist and Welfare Conservative programs are available at the 1946 start because their foundational currents predate the later speeches used to document their mature policy expression.

Agitators are ineligible only when all four strongest preferences are already satisfied; alternative strongest laws within a group are tested with OR. Every law in that grievance guard belongs to one of the character's four retained groups. The guard does not exclude ordinary officers. Only Presidential Developmentalist and Agrarian Reformer explicitly disapprove monarchy within a retained governance group; only those profiles exclude a generated reigning monarch or heir under a monarchy. The remaining profiles do not inherit a blanket anti-monarchist restriction.

The separate JSON file supplies IDs, display names, start dates, eligible interest groups, broad families, narrowly chosen party families, selected law groups, and source URLs for integration. A family is routing metadata, not a claim that the historical person joined the corresponding in-game party. Universalist Social Democrat and Liberation Theologian use progressive routing and social-democratic party affinity, following the existing democratic-socialist convention because the generic socialist helper also drives communist revolt naming. In particular Independent Unionist has no socialist or market-liberal party-family assignment: its 1980 civic-union program is not recast as either communist state unionism or the later transition economy.

Only active laws in the mod are rated; all active members of each selected group are explicit, including variants. Disabled slavery and colonial-affairs stubs are absent. Existing vanilla character icons are reused. English names and descriptions are supplied with UTF-8 BOM and CRLF endings.

## Limits and verification

The sources include contemporary programs and speeches, institutional archives, and historical accounts. Their policy claims are evidence for stated priorities, not automatic proof of implementation or outcomes. The 1959 Conservative manifesto is a reproduction of a primary program; the Guatemala US intelligence assessment reflects its authors' contemporary perspective, not a neutral verdict on every Arbenz policy.

This pack does not create environmental or reproductive-rights laws absent from the mod. General laws are used only as documented approximations. Factory councils remain subject to their existing revolutionary-only enactment restriction. The Dissident Democrat's preferred No Home Affairs law retains the existing AI prohibition on abolishing its institution; the ideology rating does not silently override that separate rule.

Static validation should check the four-group cap, active-law membership, known icons, matched IG eligibility, strongest-law grievance guards, and localization coverage/encoding. No claim of engine runtime validation or campaign balance follows from passing these checks.
