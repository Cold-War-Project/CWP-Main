# Ideology names for alternate Cold War timelines

In-game labels describe beliefs, institutions or political strategies. They do not require a named founder, a particular historical party, or a specific sequence of real-world movements. Geographic and religious terms remain where they define the existing program.

Historical figures remain in the research notes as evidence and examples. The generic names describe the selected game agenda, not a claim that the source tradition and every similarly named real-world movement are equivalent.

Only display names and four descriptions changed. Law positions, the 48-character roster, 4/3/4 law-group limits, weights, country/date gates, party associations and historical character assignments are preserved. Internal ideology IDs retain their original keys so existing references do not need migration. Family tooltips resolve these IDs through GetName and automatically display the new labels.
