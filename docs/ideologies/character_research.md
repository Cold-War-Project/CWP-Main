# Cold War character ideologies

The displayed names follow the [alternate-timeline naming guide](naming.md). Historical people and organizations below document the research basis; their existence is not implied by an in-game label.

Research checked 26 September 2026. These twelve core profiles are political archetypes derived from identifiable Cold War currents, not exhaustive biographies or a claim that promises matched conduct. The implementation follows the installed vanilla `common/ideologies/01_character_ideologies.txt`, including separate country, IG-leader and other-character selection scopes. Each profile rates four defining law groups, with every active local law included inside those groups. The 36 additional profiles are documented in the three expansion research files and the full character roster. Disabled slavery and colonial-affairs compatibility stubs are excluded.

## Selection and integration

Unrestricted profiles begin on 1 January 1946. Special eligibility filters are:

- Agrarian Communism: Han-primary countries before 1960; international selection from 1960.
- Self-Management Socialism: 1950 onward, with a South Slavic primary culture or existing cooperative ownership. This is a regional/diffusion approximation, not a nonalignment mechanic.
- Christian democracy: configured Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox or Oriental Orthodox country religion. This broad filter cannot capture every Christian minority politician in a differently configured country.
- Arab socialism: 1952 onward, with an Arab-heritage primary culture.
- African socialism: 1961 onward, with an African-heritage primary culture excluding the diaspora trait. This approximates the African decolonization current rather than determining individuals' politics from ancestry.
- Military developmentalism: 1961 onward, in the military/business/small-property constituencies; international availability represents an institutional model.
- Market conservatism: 1975 onward.

Country gates govern random generation. They do not force enactments or convert existing leaders. Both IG-leader and ordinary-character eligibility use explicit IG pools. Base IG-leader weight is 10, with 5 for the principal constituency and 10 for matching institutions; other-character weight is 10 plus 5 for the principal constituency. Weights were reduced as the roster expanded. These are initial tuning values, not measured historical probabilities. Per-profile vanilla-style NAND guards exclude agitators whose core program is already enacted, preserving ordinary officer eligibility. Profiles that explicitly oppose monarchy exclude generated reigning monarchs and heirs. Agitator goals refer only to retained groups and their strongest approved laws.

Integrated starting assignments: Stalin to Centralist Communist; Mao to Agrarian Communist with the goals-versus-current-law caveat; Attlee/Bevan to Democratic Socialist; De Gasperi to Christian Democrat; Truman to Social Liberal. Tito's self-management profile should begin no earlier than 1950. The six existing character-history definitions are assigned accordingly; their other history fields are preserved.

## Limits of the law menu

Ratings are design inferences from sources, not quotations or proof that leaders used game categories. Unspecified groups remain governed by the IG's other ideologies. Federalism, nonalignment, import-substitution versus export promotion, and ownership/management distinctions exceed the menu's precision.

National champions require interventionism. Narrow profiles do not automatically supply opinions on all related economic or institutional groups. Corporatized unions are the nearest available proxy for state-subordinated labor in Soviet/populist profiles without equating their institutions. Factory councils have a pre-existing revolutionary-country enactment restriction: Self-Management Socialist preferences do not remove it. Ideologies do not bypass technology or law prerequisites. The integrating change extends party and broad character-family checks, command-economy AI ruler eligibility, and corporatized-union AI leader eligibility. It does not relax the factory-council enactment restriction.

Static checks validate IDs, complete active local law-group coverage, localization and existing installed vanilla icons. They do not establish in-game balance, runtime scope behavior or a played campaign result.
