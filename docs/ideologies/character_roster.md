# Character roster: 48 focused Cold War currents

Each profile has exactly four law groups. The date is its earliest random availability; additional regional, religious or institutional gates are documented in the linked research. In-game names describe political programs that can emerge in an alternate timeline. Historical names in the research column are source anchors, not required founders or automatic character spawns. See the [naming guide](naming.md) for the label changes. Other law opinions continue to come from the character's IG.
