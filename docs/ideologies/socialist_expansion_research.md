# Focused socialist character expansion

The displayed names follow the [alternate-timeline naming guide](naming.md). Historical people and organizations below document the research basis; their existence is not implied by an in-game label.

Researched 26 September 2026. Each of these twelve character currents has exactly four law groups. They add a limited political agenda to an interest group rather than reproducing an entire regime. Ratings are design interpretations of the sources and may represent proposals rather than implemented policy. All referenced laws are active local definitions; every selected group includes every local law.

## Selection and integration

Base weight is 10 for both IG leaders and other characters, with 5 for the principal constituency and 10 for one matching institutional setting. Total weight per profile is capped at 25. Matching IG sets constrain both selection paths. Only the normal eight interest groups are eligible; the pre-existing ninth Bureaucrats group is excluded. Self-Reliant Socialists can draw from Armed Forces or Intelligentsia. The principal constituency bonus goes to Intelligentsia for Market Socialists and Armed Forces for Isolationist Communists and Party-State Conservatives. The agitator guard uses only strongly approved laws in the four retained groups. Generated reigning monarchs and heirs are excluded only where the profile explicitly opposes monarchy in its governance block.

Socialist Legalists, Reform Communists, Market Socialists, Democratic Communists and Party-State Conservatives require an existing council republic or command economy. Pluralist Communists require a European-heritage primary culture. Welfare Communists require Cuba or a Latin American settler primary culture; Constitutional Socialists use that Latin American regional proxy. Isolationist Communism begins in Albania and becomes internationally selectable in 1978. Self-Reliant Socialists require a Korean-heritage primary culture. Insurrectionary Socialists and Democratic Councilists have international eligibility. These are generation filters rather than historical inevitabilities, and deliberately do not assign ideology to individuals from ancestry.

Exact IDs, IG sets, dates, party families and broad integration flags are in `socialist_expansion.json`. Constitutional Socialist uses the progressive family and social_democrat party affinity because the generic socialist-family helper also drives communist-revolt and exile naming. Party-State Conservative remains socialist rather than inheriting unrelated generic conservative/reactionary behavior. These definition files do not change historical character assignments or law mechanics. Integration uses the existing party helpers and IG pools described in the README. Agrarian Communist and Party-State Conservative economic preferences remain inherited from their IG; command-economy AI compatibility preserves that path without adding a fifth law group.

National militia approximates revolutionary mobilization, factory councils approximate workers democracy, and peasant proprietorship approximates household or redistributive farming where no exact tenure law exists. Existing enactment restrictions, including the revolutionary prerequisite for factory councils, remain authoritative. Party family membership does not automatically expand the four-group opinion agenda.

## Verification

Generation asserts exactly four complete active local law groups per profile, unique ideology IDs, valid opinion levels, existing installed vanilla icons, and strongly approved retained-group grievance goals. Script and English localization use UTF-8 BOM with CRLF. Metadata is UTF-8 JSON. These checks do not establish in-game generation, balance or historical outcomes.
