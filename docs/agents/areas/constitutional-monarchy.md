# Parliamentary crowns: the Prime Minister rules, the monarch reigns

Jack, 2026-11-20: in constitutional monarchies the head of government (the Prime Minister) holds the ruler slot, and the head of state is a background character who is not part of the government, as vanilla Japan's Emperor is under the Shogunate. Branch `feat/constitutional-monarchy`. Design notes (two plans and the judge's merge) are in `~/forge/notes/const-monarchy/` on Forge.

## The vanilla model copied

Vanilla `gov_shogunate` (hereditary) seats the Shogun. The Emperor holds `character_role_emperor_of_japan` (`type = other`, `can_be_interest_group_leader = no`), the country variable `emperor_var` points at him, and script handles his succession (`character_japan_imperial_succession_chain_effect`) and his return to the throne (`country_restore_japanese_emperor`). The engine only knows heirs and regencies for `hereditary` rulers, so the background crown's succession is all script.

## Who counts

A country is a **parliamentary crown** (`cwp_country_has_parliamentary_crown`, `common/scripted_triggers/cwp_crown_triggers.txt`) when it has `law_monarchy`, any voting franchise, and the amendment `cwp_amendment_constitutional_monarchy` or `cwp_amendment_symbol_emperor`. The 1946 country history sets `cwp_crown_start_var` (first line of the block, before the law package) to bridge the start, before `common/history/global` adds the amendments; the monthly pulse removes it once the amendment is there (Japan keeps it until the Symbol Emperor amendment). Without the amendment the monarch governs (`gov_cwp_executive_monarchy`). Interest groups can sponsor or repeal the amendment, so who governs follows the politics, not the calendar.

Jack's calls with defaults (2026-11-20, open for his verdict): dominion governors-general move to the background; occupied JAP/ROM/BUL are PM-led; PER and MTB PM-led, EGY/IRQ monarch-led; the amendment's legitimacy bonus is vote-based; no politics-panel head-of-state portrait yet; "Prime Minister" for every PM type.

## Objects

- **Roles** `common/character_roles/cwp_crown_roles.txt`: `character_role_cwp_monarch`, `_crown_heir`, `_regent`, `_governor_general` (six-year term via `cwp_gg_term_var`, yearly pulse). Titles: `common/customizable_localization/cwp_crown_titles.txt` (King/Queen, Grand Duke, Emperor, Shah, Tsar; Crown Prince, Heir Presumptive, Heir to the Throne, Hereditary Grand Duke; Governor-General, Governor).
- **Country variables**: `cwp_monarch_var` (the reigning monarch, ruling or not), `cwp_crown_heir_var`, `cwp_crown_regent_var`, `cwp_gg_var`, `cwp_crown_pretender_var` (exiled ex-monarch, used for a restoration), `cwp_monarch_absent_var` (BEL and GRE 1946: the king is abroad, a regent acts), `cwp_shared_crown_var`, `cwp_crown_restore_var` (between the two type-change hooks).
- **Government types**: PM types use `parliamentary_elective` with the hooks `cwp_crown_change_to_parliamentary` / `cwp_crown_post_change_to_parliamentary`; every hereditary type uses `cwp_crown_change_to_monarch_rule` / `cwp_crown_post_change_to_monarch_rule`, which seats the background monarch (instead of vanilla's generated noble king) when the country moves to autocracy or repeals the amendment.
- **Effects** `common/scripted_effects/cwp_crown_effects.txt`: succession (heir accedes, else a member of the house is generated; a regent for a minor), `cwp_crown_depose` (abolition: royal family exiled, pretender remembered), `cwp_crown_abdicate`, `cwp_crown_save_monarch_scope = { NAME = x }` (use this, not `ruler`, when content means the monarch), `cwp_crown_set_house_name`, `cwp_crown_sync`.
- **On_actions** `common/on_actions/cwp_crown_on_actions.txt`: death of a crown character, abolition (`on_law_activated`), coming of age (regency ends), monthly sync, yearly governor-general check.
- **Events** `events/cwp_crown_events.txt`: `cwp_crown.1` accession, `cwp_crown.2` end of regency (player only, one option each: they formalise a fact).
- **Roster**: codes `M`, `MH`, `RG`, `GG` in `tools/character_roster/gen.py`; tags in `tags.py` (`CROWN`, `DOMINION`). The 1946 ruling IG sets were chosen (CAN Liberals only, NET adds the intelligentsia) so the strongest ruling IG is led by the real Prime Minister (Shidehara now leads the Bureaucrats; Attlee stays with the Educated Professionals, the strongest IG of the Labour coalition in game). New characters: Baudouin (BEL), Crown Prince Paul (GRE), Todor Pavlov (BUL regency council).

## Content that means "the monarch"

Uses of `ruler`/`heir` that meant the monarch were changed: the institutional question (`cwp_iq_proclaim_republic`, the heir button, the monarch scope in the referendum events), Belgium's Royal Question (abdication now exiles Leopold, not the Prince Regent; the king's return ends the regency), portrait dress (`cwp_portrait_character_monarch` uses `cwp_crown_is_royal`), the ideology guards that stop a monarch advocating a republic, the Scandinavia dynamic name, the republic laws' AI weight (a socialist PM no longer counts as a republican ruler), and `resign_from_office` (a PM can resign). Before adding content about a monarch, use `cwp_crown_save_monarch_scope` or `cwp_crown_is_royal`.

## Monarchy tradition (Jack, 2026-11-22)

Only countries with a monarchy tradition may enact `law_monarchy`, and its `can_impose` refuses a target without one (`scope:target_country`; root is the imposer), so that a regime change led by a monarchy does not crown a king where there never was one. `cwp_crown_has_monarchy_tradition` (country): a current `law_monarchy` (exact law, not colonial administration), `cwp_crown_tradition_var` (set in `common/history/global/cwp_crown_1946.txt` for every 1946 monarchy, ALB YUG HUN, and the native monarchies under colonial rule LBY MYY BST ESW ZZB BRU BUG RWD), or a pretender in exile (realms of the shared British crown count only while they keep it). The tradition is dynamic (Jack, 2026-11-22): any activation of `law_monarchy` (decolonization, restoration, revolution, regime change) sets the variable for good, so a country that loses its crown keeps it, and vanilla's royalist movements (`movement_royalist_constitutional`, `movement_royalist_absolutist`, in the same-name overwrite `common/political_movements/00_ideological_movements.txt`) can now form there to campaign for a restoration. Whether the engine's regime change consults `can_impose` is not confirmed; a fallback must test the target before the change, since an installed monarchy records a tradition at once. Royal claims with a monarchist movement in the period count too (Jack, 2026-11-22), by tag in the trigger: FRA POR AUS GER RUS BRZ DAI. Spain is a monarchy in 1946.

Vanilla's "Stamp Out Monarchism" (`je_abolish_monarchy`, `REPLACE:` in `common/journal_entries/zz_vanilla_journal_entries.txt`), opened when a country leaves the monarchy law, ends the tradition when it completes: five years without a powerful interest group that wants the crown back removes `cwp_crown_tradition_var` and the pretender, sets `cwp_crown_republic_settled_var` (which also cancels a tag's royal claim), and the royalist movements disband (their `disband_trigger`). If a monarchy is activated again later, the tradition returns and the settled flag is cleared.

## Restoring the proper dynasty (Jack, 2026-11-22)

When a crown comes back, the rightful claimant takes it rather than a generated noble. A deposed monarch of this game comes home from exile (`cwp_crown_pretender_var`). For houses without a throne in 1946, `common/character_templates/cwp_crown_claimants.txt` (plus `VNM_bao_dai` from the 1946 roster) lists the heads of house in order: RUS/RSF (Vladimir Kirillovich, then Maria Vladimirovna; Vladimir's claim was disputed), FRA (Henri, Count of Paris, then his son Henri; the Orleanist line), POR (Duarte Nuno, then Duarte Pio), AUS and HUN (Otto von Habsburg, then Karl; one person for both crowns), GER (Crown Prince Wilhelm to 1951, Louis Ferdinand, then Georg Friedrich), BRZ (Pedro Henrique of the Vassouras branch, then Luiz), DAI (Bao Dai, then Bao Long), ALB (Zog I, then Leka), YUG (Peter II, then Alexander). `cwp_crown_create_claimant = { MODE = ruler|monarch }` takes the first one alive on the date (death dates in the effect): a character already in the game, at home, abroad or in exile, is brought home; otherwise the template is created. Under a Prime-Minister government (`cwp_crown_ensure_monarch`) a member of the house is generated when no claimant applies (house names in `cwp_crown_set_house_name`). Under a monarch-ruled government, `on_law_activated` for `law_monarchy` (after the start) sets `cwp_crown_restoration_check_var`; the next monthly pulse seats the claimant in place of a generated ruler (`cwp_crown_restore_dynasty`; a monarch brought back by the restoration hook is marked `cwp_crown_restored_var` and kept). Untested in game: that `on_law_activated` fires for a won revolution or a country formed with the law, and the engine's succession when the claimant later dies (the heir is whatever the engine picks).
