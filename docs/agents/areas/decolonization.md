# Decolonization

Design note for the decolonization content. Proposal: [docs/agents/proposals/decolonization.md](../proposals/decolonization.md) (PR #786). Jack picked **option C first, then option B** for India, Indonesia, Indochina and Algeria (2026-11-17).

## How it works

Everything rides on the 1.15 subject system. Colonies in the 1946 start are subjects (puppet, protectorate, dominion) of the metropoles, and a subject with **liberty desire** of 90 or more can use the engine's independence war goal. The content moves liberty desire and opens negotiated exits; the engine and the AI do the rest. Nothing is scheduled by date.

### Option C: The Winds of Change (`je_cwp_winds_of_change`)

A contextless JE, active from day one, involving the USA, the USSR, every tracked colony and every metropole that still has one.

- **Tracked colonies**: subjects of GBR, FRA, NET, BEL, POR, SPA, ITA, USA, AST, NZL, SAF that are not military occupations or settler dominions (CAN, AST, NZL, SAF, NEW). Registered once, when the JE opens (`cwp_decolonization_register_colonies`): subjects created later, such as Cold War satellites or Japan after its occupation, are not colonies. Tracked colonies and their metropoles are added to the JE each month if not already involved. Variables: `cwp_colony`, `cwp_metropole`, `cwp_former_colony`, `cwp_colonial_power`.
- **Anti-colonial tide bar** (`cwp_tide_bar`, 0 to 100, start 10). Rises with each independence (+4, read the month after it happens), free former colonies (+0.03 each, capped at +0.6/month), colonies at war with their overlord (+0.3 each), superpower stances (+0.3 each champion, -0.2 each guardian). Falls by 4 for each rising crushed by a case JE.
- **Tiers** at 25/50/75/100 put `cwp_anticolonial_tide` (+0.2 weekly liberty desire per tier; at +0.06 colonies drifted down to 5-25 liberty desire in the 2026-11-17 observer run) on every tracked colony.
- **Buttons**: superpowers champion self-determination or stand by allied empires; metropoles fund development, promise self-government, declare an emergency, or transfer power to colonies at 75+ liberty desire; colonies run a nationalist campaign or appeal to a backing superpower. Cooldowns are variables (`<button>_cd`); the engine has no button cooldown field.
- Independence by any road is detected monthly and fires `cwp_decolonization.1` (new state picks a Cold War orientation) and `.2` (the metropole).

## Why the historical path is the likely one

- India: Labour in government, British debt and India's liberty desire push self-rule to 100 in about two years; communal tension starts close to the partition line and only sustained Congress-League talks keep it below.
- Indonesia: the Republic's hold on Java and Sumatra slowly raises pressure; police actions push it back until the AI US answers one with aid pressure, after which Dutch war support drains and the war or the Round Table ends Dutch rule.
- Indochina: the people's war phase keeps the war going for years; the Chinese sanctuary and aid outpace French reinforcements once the PRC is at peace, US aid slows that, and a French AI with debt or a left government takes the settlement once the bar passes +25, so the likely ending is partition.
- Algeria: the FLN only rises after Indochina and the neighbours change the picture, and French will erodes with the length of the war.

All of this depends on the 1946 start state (governments, debt, subject types) and on AI weights, so the observer run is what confirms it.

## Colonial wars as real wars (2026-11-18)

The military thread's 1946-49 runs showed the DRV defaulting in June 1946 and the Indonesian Republic in October 1946, their armies collapsing. Changes (branch feat/decolonization-guerrilla):
- `cwp_guerrilla_war` (military goods -50%, loan interest -50%, supply consumption -50%, morale loss -15%, army defense +25%, training rate +100%, casualty recovery +25%; no wage cut, since half-paid barracks stayed nearly empty; while it is on, `wanted_army_size_script_value` targets at least 1 barracks level per 1M incorporated pops, max 15, and skips the peacetime affordability cuts) on DAI and IDS while their JEs run, and on any tracked colony at war with its overlord (Winds of Change monthly update; removed when the war ends).
- Chinese aid also puts `cwp_foreign_military_aid` on DAI (military goods -25%, army offense +5%, 12 months).
- Escalation in Indochina: once the DRV is a French subject with liberty desire 75+ and not at war, `cwp_indochina.3` (the accords break down; Ho Chi Minh's Appeal for National Resistance) lets it open a `dp_independence` play on France, the vanilla Raj pattern, or return to talks (-10 liberty desire). At most once a year (`cwp_indochina_haiphong_cd`). The AI goes to war mostly from liberty desire 85 or with Chinese aid.
- The accords breakdown also fires when the DRV is independent and the 1946 confrontation has stayed a play for 6 months (military thread's combined run, 2026-11-19: no fighting by 1958). Then the DRV ends the play and opens a `dp_return_state` war on VNM for Annam; France joins as overlord.
- Indonesia won independence on 1946-06-20 in that run, before any fighting, through the Round Table. The Round Table now also needs a Dutch police action and 18 months of war (`cwp_indonesia_war_months`), and US pressure needs a police action first (the Republic's diplomacy only makes Washington more willing).
- Run fa0825cb7 (2026-11-19): the NET-IDS war ran from 1946 into 1949 as intended, but the Indochina JE closed in mid-1946 with the 'French Union holds' outcome: FRA's 1946 make_dominion play became a short war that ended with the DRV a French dominion (the March 1946 accords), which the JE counted as a French victory, so the DRV's resistance modifier went and the FRA-DAI war only came in October 1949. Fix (branch fix/indochina-war-pacing): `cwp_indochina_war_fought` is set only by a war the DRV fights while a French subject; the accords event now goes to war at AI weight 25 (+25 at liberty desire 80, +25 at 90) against 15 for more talks.
- Run e32f10826 (2026-11-19): the JE stayed open as intended, but the DRV's liberty desire only climbed from 64 to 80 in 19 months (the engine's own factors swamp +1.5 a week), so the accords broke down only in mid-1948, and the war of independence ended within months with France annexing the DRV (the counter war goal was annex_country). Fix (branch fix/indochina-war-pacing-2): +3 liberty desire a month for the DRV while it is a French subject at peace; the accords event at 70; France's counter war goal is reduce_autonomy, so a lost war of independence keeps the DRV alive and the struggle can resume; the French-victory outcome also needs the DRV's liberty desire below 25; `cwp_guerrilla_war` army defense +25% and +2% conscription.
- To check in an observer run: neither insurgent state defaults by 1948; a France-DRV war exists by about 1947 and Indonesian independence comes no earlier than about 1948 in a median run.

## Opening the journal entries

Contextless journal entries never open from a `possible` block: the 2026-11-17 observer run (1946-1956) showed none of the five active. Each is added by `add_contextless_journal_entry` from `cwp_decolonization_monthly_pulse` (`common/on_actions/cwp_decolonization_on_actions.txt`, chained into `on_monthly_pulse`, the China and Korea pattern) when its `cwp_<x>_can_open` trigger holds (`woc`, `india`, `indonesia`, `indochina`, `algeria`, in `cwp_decolonization_triggers.txt`). Each JE sets the global `cwp_<x>_active` in `immediate` and clears it in every outcome; the Winds of Change also sets `cwp_woc_concluded` when it ends. The JEs have no `possible` or `is_shown_when_inactive` blocks.

## War support hook

War support effects are **condition-based**, not pulsed. `war_support_from_journal_entries` (vanilla `common/script_values/war_support_values.txt`, summed into each beat's war support change) is overridden once, as a hand-written `REPLACE:` above the generated marker in `common/script_values/zz_vanilla_script_values.txt`. It keeps vanilla's Russian civil war block and adds one line per area: `add = cwp_decolonization_war_support` (defined in `common/script_values/cwp_decolonization_values.txt`) and `add = cwp_china_war_support` (a placeholder in `cwp_chinese_civil_war_values.txt`, owned by the China thread). Root is the country, `scope:war` the war. Since the 2026-11-18 rework the Indochina and Indonesia JEs have no bars: buttons set short variables (reinforcements, aid, police actions, guerrilla war, US pressure) that this value reads, plus the war's length for French war weariness. Don't call `add_war_support_change` on a pulse: it is a persistent rate and stacks (advisor, 2026-11-17). War support runs 0-100, red band at 25, and native per-beat factors reach about -5.

## DRV finances: taxes in the liberated zones (2026-11-22)

Batch d729 (after the military thread's fix/indochina-viet-bac) showed the cause of the DRV's collapse: it collected almost no tax (income and poll taxes empty under land-based taxation, about 3k a week against 10-16k of wages, goods and interest), defaulted in 1949.10, capitulated, and as a French puppet at liberty desire 100 never rebuilt (about 2k men in every later round). The real DRV fought on rice levies and contributions in kind from the villages it held (the agricultural tax of 1951 formalized them), which a money economy built on market output does not see.

- `cwp_indochina_liberated_zone_taxes` (static modifier, `country_tax_income_add = 1`) is added to DAI by the Indochina JE's monthly pulse for 12 months at a time, with `multiplier = cwp_indochina_liberated_zone_taxes_value` (1,000 a week per million inhabitants, min 500; about 9,900 for the 9.9 million of 1946, enough to cover a wartime budget of 10-12k a week before interest). It runs whenever the JE is open, at war or at peace (inside the French Union, as a puppet, or independent while France still holds Vietnamese states), because the DRV levied in all of these. The amount is fixed for 12 months at a time and follows the population the DRV holds, so it shrinks with lost ground, down to a floor of 500. Removed by `cwp_indochina_cleanup`.
- The same modifier has `country_loan_interest_rate_mult = -0.5` (summed with `cwp_guerrilla_war`'s -0.5 it cancels the DRV's interest, unless an unrecognized rank adds +0.5 to +1.0; batch 3ea9 showed no interest paid): the DRV financed its deficits mainly by issuing its own currency (the Ho banknotes), with some national loans and resistance bonds. Batch 199e (levies only, 3bb83ae5a) showed why: the non-interest budget roughly balanced (about 13.8k of income against 12.5k of costs in 1952), but the debt run up in 1947-49 reached 2.3M, its interest 12.9k a week, and the DRV defaulted in 1953.4. In that run the first war ended in 1948, the DRV won back protectorate status in 1949, fought France from 1950.9 to 1954.7 (French war support down to 83) and was a puppet again after the 1954 round; the PRC existed.
- Not changed: the Chinese aid button only works if the PRC exists. In 5 of the 6 latest observer runs to 1952 the ROC won the Chinese civil war, so the historical turning point of 1950 (Chinese aid and the Border Campaign) is missing. That is the Chinese civil war's outcome, not this JE's; with an ROC victory a longer French war is a legible deviation.
- To check in an observe run: DAI not in default by 1950, its army above about 20k men between rounds, and a France-DRV war that lasts into the early 1950s.
- Batch 3ea9 (9db721133, levies plus interest cut; ROC won the civil war): the DRV left the French Union in 1947 and stayed independent to 1956, fought France from 1948.5 to 1952 (its own war support down to 68) and again in 1953-55, but the AI spent the extra money on a larger army (military wages about 20k a week against 14k of income) and defaulted in 1951.3 anyway. French war support stayed at 100 throughout, because the people's-war +3 a week went to both sides. Changed: the +3 (and the resistance +5) is now DRV-only, and France's "dirty war" -1 starts at 24 months of war (was 36) and "endless war" -1 at 48 (was 72). French reinforcements and American aid (+1 each) offset them, so French staying power is a choice the player and the AI make.
- Batch a3b9 (673bf22b6, all of the above; ROC won the civil war): the DRV lost the first round in 1947-48 and was a French puppet from 1948 to 1958, but fought eight rounds (1948.7, 1949.11 to 1950, 1951.2, 1952.5 to 1953, 1954.8 to 1955, 1955.12, 1957.9) instead of short ones; it defaulted in 1953.3 and 1955.12, and France never lost Vietnam. Each round is a new autonomy play, and French war support starts each one at 100, so the 24/48-month drains rarely bite before a round ends. Across batches 199e, 3ea9 and a3b9 the DRV is far more durable than before (one run independent 1947-56, one with a 1950-54 war), but no run reaches a French withdrawal. What is left: a PRC victory in the civil war (Chinese aid, the 1950 turning point), and what a French win in a round means (a puppet DRV restarts each round from full French war support).
