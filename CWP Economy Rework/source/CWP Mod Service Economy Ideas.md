Instead of relying on a single services good to represent service based economies, and the level of services seen throughout the various stages of a country's economic development from a low income country to a high income country, services needs to be broken up into different levels which operate on different market access levels with different trading restrictions. These would be Local Services, National Services, and International Services.

Local Services:
- Local Good: Yes
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: No
- Buildings:
  - Local Services Sector
  - National Retail Sector
- Designed to represent small local businesses in the economy - hairdressers, salons, corner shops, and other small local businesses, etc.
- The good should be in high proportional demand at lower SoL levels, decreasing as a pop's SoL level increases. Ideal behaviour is that countries that are starting to develop (low income countries) should produce and consume a large amount of local services to partially represent their disconnection from the interconnected global economy.
- There should always be some demand for local services no matter the SoL living (even rich pops have the occasional use for these types of services, but never 0 - though perhaps 0 for the ultra-wealthy if we want to model it to that extent).
- Should also be produced in a small amount (via a secondary PMG?) in National Retail Sector buildings, to reflect that some national level service companies continue to provide local level services, though at a lower priority compared to the national level they would be focusing on (the primary PMG)

National Services:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: No
- Buildings:
  - National Retail Sector
  - Global Retail Conglomerates
- Designed to represent national scale businesses in the economy - supermarket chains, restaurant chains, etc - the level of services that operate within a country's market, but doesn't operate in other markets.
- The good should start at 0 or low proportional demand at lower SoL levels, increasing as a pop's SoL level increases. Ideal behaviour is that countries in the middle of developing (middle income countries) should be consuming services that are produced and consumed on a national level, reducing their consumption of local services as a pop's income increases and they start to become wealthier.
- There should be a small demand for National Services at low SoL levels, increasing to becoming the main source of service demand for a pop for the majority of SoL levels for middle income countries and high income countries.
- Should also be produced in a small amount (via a secondary PMG) in the Global Retail Conglomerates, to reflect the companies that have nearly finished transitioning from being a national level business/company into becoming a multinational business, though at a lower priority compared to the national level they would be focusing on (the primary PMG)

International Services:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: Yes
- Buildings:
  - Global Retail Conglomerates
  - National Retail Sector
- Designed to represent international scale businesses in the national and international economy - multinational franchises such as McDonalds, Pizza Hut, Starbucks, etc. This level of service operates on a national level and an international level.
- The good should be designed to work with the same values as the National Services good, but is made a separate good to allow countries to apply tariffs & subventions to allow for some market control over how their country's pops fulfil their service demand; some countries would want to protect their own service sector from foreign competition, while others would want to encourage the export of their country's services to gain market share in foreign markets.
- There should be 0 demand for international services at low SoL levels as the pops are unable to afford international services, which slowly starts to increase as the SoL level increases, reaching equality with the national services good in the middle SoL levels, before international services become the preferred service demand source as pops reach high SoL levels.
- Should be produced in a small amount (via a secondary PMG) in the National Retail Sector, to reflect the companies that are just starting to expand into foreign markets, reflecting the companies beginning the process of trying to transition into becoming multinational companies/businesses - though the good should be produced at a lower priority compared to the international level they would be focussing on (the primary PMG)

Professional Advisory & Consulting:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: Yes
- Buildings:
  - Professional Services Firms
- Designed to represent the professional services sector - professional advisory, consulting, management, etc - from companies such as the big four in London or McKinsey & Company in the USA as examples.
- Operate on the national and international level, representing that countries would see the use of their own country's management/consulting companies or foreign management/consulting companies, as this sector of the economy is where countries reach the desired end goal of a service based economy, such as the USA and the UK.
- Used mostly in the production methods for other buildings throughout the economy, but could also find a fitting place as a pop demand for pops with high SoL levels, to represent that class of pops needing to employ professional level services to assist with managing their assets, managing their life and assisting them in their lives, etc.


These goods use the urbanization mechanic as a key component of their function, which will require urbanization to somehow reflect the population of a state and how wealthy the pops in that state are. The urban center would be repurposed to represent 4 key population demands that have an effect on the economy - especially a developed service based economy. These 4 demands are:
- Healthcare
- Education
- Stability/Security
- Housing

These 4 demands are all paid for by a pop to acquire enough of the demand to meet their needs as per their SoL - the question is generally where the supply of that demand comes from; on a scale from fully public/state owned in communist economies or capitalist countries that have decided to use a fully socialised model, to a completely capitalist economy where the state isn't involved in the supply of the demand at all. Each of these 4 demands have sources that can either be public, private, or somewhere in-between. If we abstract these demands into broad models, we would get the following:
- Healthcare: Public Healthcare, Private Healthcare, and Public-Private Mix Healthcare
- Education: Public Education, Private Education, and Public-Private Mix Education
- Stability/Security: Public Law Enforcement, Private Law Enforcement, and Public-Private Mix Law Enforcement
- Housing: State-owned housing, fully private housing, and state-owned/private mix housing

Due to the goods I'm proposing to add, as well as their connected new buildings, and combined with modifying how much urbanization in a state is required for each level of urban center, it will take some work to balance how much urbanization should come from each building in the mod, but I think we might be able to find a sweet spot where this could work.


Healthcare is a difficult area to represent, as the good primarily needs to function as a local good for the pops that live in the state, yet the source of healthcare can vary based on how public/private its source is, and whether a pop would acquire it locally or pay to acquire it in a different country (medical tourism). As such, on a market level healthcare needs to be split into two goods: Healthcare Supply and Healthcare Demand. Pops will have a healthcare pop need, scaled to the amount of Healthcare each SoL level would expect to have/use, which can only be fulfilled via the Healthcare Demand good.

The Healthcare demand good is made in a building that operates in what was the urban center building that is repurposed to function as a representation for the non-economic service demand. It would use a production method that contains no workers/employees, instead acting as a simple converter that converts Healthcare Supply (a non-local and tradeable good) into Healthcare Demand (a local non-tradeable good), which scales based on the urbanization in the state. Pops will have a healthcare pop demand, which can only be fulfilled by consuming the Healthcare demand good, and the healthcare demand good is made by converting the healthcare supply good into it. The healthcare supply good is made in two buildings: Public Healthcare Provider and Private Healthcare Provider.

Healthcare Demand would need to have a low price - I'd suggest using a price of 1 so that the +/- % change in price can have market effects while still being easy for our modelling/calculations by assuming that a price of 1 is the base for the calucations (that healthcare isn't in short supply and isn't abundant), and then representing a pop's demand for healthcare based on its SoL level - and the ratio that Healthcare Supply is converted into Healthcare Demand should be 1 to 1. This would act as a way to put a cost on the pop for healthcare and create a 1 to 1 demand for healthcare in the national (and potentially world) market while keeping it tied to the state on a local level to represent pop demand in the state. What this allows us to do, is model the effects of pop unrest if there is insufficient healthcare supply, as pops will have to pay more to maintain their SoL level or have it decrease, which creates radicals/unrest and has economic effects on the wider economy. We could then use an on_action to check the price of the Healthcare Demand good in a state, and on a monthly check update a modifier that would affect the state's birth rate and the state's mortality rate (either 1 modifer covering both or two separate modifiers - one for each), based on whether there is a lack of sufficient healthcare (price is above 0%) or whether there is plenty of healthcare (price is below 0%). 

Healthcare Supply is made in either the Public Healthcare Provider or the Private Healthcare Provider. The base price of Healthcare Supply should also be 1, or at least be the same as the Healthcare Supply/Healthcare Demand converter ratio; which should mean that there is no additional cost in the urban center PM as it will just be acting as a converter and has no workers to pay. As pops pay for their healthcare demand, and the urban center PM uses that money to buy the amount of healthcare supply that will be converted into that healthcare demand, this allows us to model how people would pay for healthcare based on the system used in the country, as this money will be the money that Healthcare Supply providers earn by supplying the healthcare supply. This money is used to pay for the wages, required input goods, etc, with losses being paid for by the state if it is the Public Healthcare Provider, or by reserves of the Private Healthcare Provider (which would lead to building levels being removed if it can't make a profit). The profits will end up either in the country's treasury if it is the Public Healthcare Provider, or as profits paid out to the owners of the Private Healthcare Providers.

The Public Healthcare Provider would function as a state owned and funded supplier of the Healthcare Supply good, working on a similar principle to state owned ports. This would allow us to capture the financial cost of running a public healthcare system, as the government would need to pay the difference for the wages of all the involved workers as well as paying for the medical supplies, such as pharmaceuticals and other goods, if the money coming from the Healthcare Demand/Supply conversion isn't enough to cover the costs.

The Private Healthcare Provider would function like any other private business, except with the good output being the Healthcare Supply good. This would allow us to model how private healthcare is provided to pops (an abstracted version), and how to create a profit seeking behaviour in healthcare; which would hopefully provide natural economic behaviour such as shutting down/downsizing facilities if the building is making a loss so a profit can be restored, and that money is taken out of the healthcare system to be paid out as profit to investors/building owners.

The amount of the Healthcare Supply good produced would be based on the number of buildings, the PMs used, and potentially whether any is imported from abroad (which would model pops going to other countries for healthcare treatment, while the pop in game remains in its state). We could make the automation PMG for these two buildings represent what level of staffing these buildings are running at, with the starting PM being 100% staffing levels, with % increases or decreases for the other production methods (130%, 120%, 110%, 100%, 90%, 80%, 70%, etc) with proportional increase/decrease in the number of jobs, required goods input values, and goods output values.

Healthcare Supply:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: Yes
- Buildings:
  - Public Healthcare Provider
  - Private Healthcare Provider

Healthcare Demand:
- Local Good: Yes
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: No
- Building:
  - Healthcare Sector (Using the urban center level per amount of urbanization in a state mechanic - shown as an urban center PMG)


Note: Literacy rate is based on a pop's education access, with 100% education access being equal to 100% literacy.

Education in some ways is similar to healthcare, in that each pop has an education requirement to participate in the economy, which the game represents through literacy rate and whether pops have qualifications. As a pop's literacy rate is based solely on the amount of education access that pop has, by controlling the source of education access to be primarily dependent on how well the population's education demand is supplied by the education supply available to those pops, we can potentially model pops using foreign education institutions (universities, colleges, etc), along with the knock on effects of having an insufficient education system to support a high income country's economy, etc - not taking into account any temporary bonuses that might be given based on in-campaign content such as events, journal entries, etc.

Like with healthcare, we can use the urban centers & urbanization mechanic to act as a representation for the total amount of education demand the pops in a state have, as well as allowing us to model the education demand as a local non-tradeable good while education supply is a non-local and tradeable good; the education PMG in the urban center working as a goods converter with no jobs given. To represent the effect of a country's literacy rate and how much effort/money is put towards meeting the education demand, the literacy rate could be treated as a result of the difference in price of the education demand good between it's base price of 1 and the actual locak market price based on supply and demand. 

We could use a set literacy rate to act as the base value when a country has just enough education supply to meet the set demands of the population's education demand, but not an abundant amount; so at 0% price difference compared to the base price, the natural literacy rate would be 60% (as an example for this suggestion). We could then model insufficient education supply (the price difference being +%) as reducing literacy rate in the population (where a +75% reduces the education access by 40%), while abundant education supply (the price difference being -%) as increasing literacy rate in the population (where a -75% increases the education access by 40%); this would act as a representation of the amount of resources that is invested by a country either directly (public) or indirectly (private) into having an educated population and workforce (with qualifications).

Education Demand would come from being converted in the urban center from Education Supply into Education Demand (the 1:1 conversion with no workers involved). Similar principle to how Healthcare Demand functions.

Education Supply would be created by a public and private education provider, which operates on a similar principle to how the private and public healthcare providers function. It would operate on a national level with no trading between markets, as we generally don't see enough pops getting their primary and secondary education outside of the state they reside in. While Education supply would function similarly to how Healthcare Supply functions, this doesn't best represent tertiary education, where we observe people going to other countries to attend their universities/colleges; to represent this, we would create a Higher Education Supply which would be a tradeable good, and would be a direct pop demand for pops of middle to high SoL levels - bypassing the urban center mechanic. This Higher Education Supply would only be created in universities, which while effectively selling to the pops in their home market, also compete globally to acquire international students to attend their universities.

- Tradeable: No
- Buildings:

  - Private Education Providers

Education Demand:

  - Education Sector (Using the urban center level per amount of urbanization in a state mechanic - shown as an urban center PMG)

Higher Education Supply:

- Buildings:
  - Universities


Stability/Security is the weird and unusual thing to try and model as part of the service economy, but I would argue that developed countries/high income countries require sufficient stability/security to exist in the country for pops (and companies indirectly through those pops) to invest into economic enterprises that have hign investment costs. Furthermore, pops pay for this stability/security, either through the government or via private security, whether on top of the government provided stability/security or as a replacement for its absence. This need can be modelled as a pop demand like healthcare and education. This would also allow us to model and represent the situation in other countries where a breakdown in stability/security has led to pops paying for private security for their own protection - countries like South Africa - or to represent how a country with a lack of stability/security - such as Haiti - has a failed economy, partly because there is no stability/security for that economy to use.

We could represent the sources of this stability/security through a number of sources, to reflect the different ways that countries use to maintain stability/security - and those different ways can have different secondary side effects that could influence the country politically (as an example). The first source would be a police force, the vast majority being state-owned/controlled which practically every country has - but it is not impossible that a country could end up relying on a private police force, empowered by the state, to provide stability/security for the country - and that private police force would have a profit motive to maximise profits for the investors/owners; this would also allow us to technically include private security companies through an indirect representation. We could also include a state's intelligence apparatus which would be state owned & controlled, yet provide a source of stability/security, though perhaps with risky or painful political/social implications depending on what sort of intelligence apparatus a country is using - the USA's CIA/FBI, the UK's MI5/MI6, the USSR's NKVD, etc. It would also be possible to make it so that land battalions act as a source of stability/security for the country, though perhaps less efficiently when compared to more dedicated sources of stability/security, with the amount of stability/security coming from the army being dependent on the PMs used in the barracks (using the training PMs or adding a new PMG to represent how the army is used by the country internally?).

Like healthcare, two new goods would be added, Stability Demand and Stability Supply. Stability Demand would come from pops and be managed through the urban center like healthcare demand and education demand, using 0 workers and having the urban center PMG act as a converter of Stability Supply into Stability Demand. Like the other demand goods, Stability Demand would be a local good. Like Healthcare Demand and Education Demand, the price of the good would be 1, and we could use the price difference compared to base price (+%/-%) to apply modifiers to the state that would reflect what the effect of having insufficient stability/security or abundant stability/security in a state would represent: increased/decreased radicals & unrest, extra authority for the government to use(?), etc.

Stability Supply would come from the various sources: public/private police forces, private military companies, intelligence services, and the army. It would not be a local good and it would be tradeable - to reflect how private military companies, private security companies, etc, can sell their services nationally as well as internationally.

Stability Supply:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: Yes
- Buildings:
  - Public Police force
  - Private Police force
  - Private Military Company
  - Intelligence Service
  - Barracks

Stability Demand:
- Local Good: Yes
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: No
- Buildings:
  - Security Sector


Housing (*The Housing Theory of Everything* represent!) has become a significant part of the economies of many countries, and has been responsible in some form either directly or indirectly or some major economic events during the cold war period and afterwards, so not representing it feels like doing a disservice to modern economies and the potential to create a more interconnected economy. Like the other major demands, housing would be represented by two goods: Housing Demand and Housing Supply. Like the other major demands, the urban center & urbanization mechanic would be used to represent the demand for housing in the state, while allowing the housing supply to be more flexible, with the urban center PMG being a 1:1 converter.

Housing Supply would be created either in publicly owned housing or privately owned housing. Publicly owned housing would represent social housing, where the local government (state-owned) owns housing that the pops would pay rent towards; while Privately Owned Housing would be privately owned and have a profit motive to extract profit. I haven't quite figured out how to represent the pops owning their own property, but considering pops can't own stuff individually, and that the in-game pop represents a varying sized section of a population, it would probably be sufficient to only model if housing is privately owned or publicly owned.

Similar to the other big demands (healthcare, education, stability/security), Housing Demand would have a base price of 1, with the (+%/-%) difference compared to base price potentially triggering different modifiers at various strengths to represent different consequences depending on whether there is a shortage of housing or a surplus of housing - such as increased radicalism, homelessness, etc.

  - Privately Owned Housing
  - Publicly Owned Housing

Housing Demand:

  - Housing Sector

Research is the interesting section of the economy, and I think to make it work we would need to reconsider how much innovation technologies cost to research, as well as how tech spread is earned. I propose that we step away from the idea that universities are the only source of innovation, and that a cap on innovation generation should even exist. I propose introducing a new Research good, which is consumed by the buildings that create innovation, and is produced in any and all buildings were it could be argued that some form of innovation/research and development is occuring; innovation should come from various buildings throughout the economy, with the amount per building per level being dependent on which building it is.

As an example, each of the service buildings such produce the new research good, with the amount generated being very different: local service buildings producing very small amounts of the research good, national service buildings producing reasonable amounts of research good, and international service buildings creating more of the new research good than national service buildings. The research good should not be a local good, but it needs to be non-tradeable - research being shared should come from tech spread.

This research good would be consumed in buildings were the innovation comes from in the modern economy and throughout the cold war - examples being: barracks & naval bases (using a new PM to represent the military funding new technologies for itself), universities (requiring more of the research good as input than they output), national service buildings and international service buildings, heavy industry buildings (such as aircraft manufactories, automobile manufactories, consumer electonics manufactories, etc).

There should be no maximum innovation cap (or set it to an extremely large number so that it is effectively meaningless), as richer countries with larger economies could fund more research and innovation - they weren't artificially held back from producing more than a varying innovation cap based on the literacy rate; the literacy rate should primarily be how we control how many pops can earn qualifications. This would require us to greatly increase the innovation cost of technologies, but I don't see this as an actual negative considering the time period the mod is meant to represent.

Research:
- Local Good: No
- Fixed Price: No
- Tradeable: No
- Buildings:
  - Universities
  - Various buildings throughout the economy
  - Barracks & Naval Bases
