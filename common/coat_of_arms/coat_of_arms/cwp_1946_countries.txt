# Cold War Project: coats of arms for the countries on the map at 1946.1.1.
# Only flags vanilla does not already draw correctly are defined here; the rest are vanilla keys,
# chosen per tag in common/flag_definitions/zzz_cwp_1946_flag_definitions.txt.
# Keys: cwp_<TAG>_1946 for the start-date flag, cwp_<TAG>_<variant> for later alternatives.
# British colonial flags are Blue (or Red) Ensigns: the Union Flag canton is drawn here as a sub,
# so they do not depend on the "subject flags" game rule. Badges use vanilla emblems and are simplified.

############################################################
# Europe
############################################################
