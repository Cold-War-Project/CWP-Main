## 3. Resolution 181 (29 Nov 1947): 33 Yes, 13 No, 10 Abstain, 1 absent (C)

- **Yes:** Australia, Belgium, Bolivia, Brazil, Byelorussian SSR, Canada, Costa Rica, Czechoslovakia, Denmark, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, France, Guatemala, Haiti, Iceland, Liberia, Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Norway, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Poland, South Africa, Sweden, Ukrainian SSR, USA, USSR, Uruguay, Venezuela.
- **No:** Afghanistan, Cuba, Egypt, Greece, India, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, Yemen.
- **Abstain:** Argentina, Chile, China, Colombia, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Mexico, UK, Yugoslavia.
- **Absent:** Siam (its delegate's credentials were withdrawn).
- **Vote postponed** from 26 to 29 Nov by filibuster; on 26 Nov it would probably have failed (C).

Lobbying reports (strength varies):
- Philippines: Romulo recalled after calls from Washington (C, as reported).
- Haiti: a reported loan (D).
- France and Benelux: hints that US aid was at risk (C, as reported).
- Liberia: Firestone pressure (C, as reported).
- Central America: United Fruit (D).
- US Senate telegram; Lovett on alleged bribes and threats (C, as reported).
- India: Nehru reported bribe attempts and threats (C, as reported).
- Cuba: voted No despite pressure (C).

Plan terms:
- Area: Arab state 42.88%, Jewish state 56.47%, corpus separatum (Jerusalem-Bethlehem) 0.65% (C).
- Population: Jewish state 498k Jews / 407k Arabs (+~90k Bedouin); Arab state 725k Arabs / 10k Jews; international zone 100k Jews / 105k Arabs (C).
- Economic union with customs, currency, railways and ports, and a Joint Economic Board (C exists / P details).
- Jerusalem: UN governor, demilitarised, 10-year review (P).
- Independence by 1 Oct 1948 (P).

Additional rare-path candidates from the research:
1. **Morrison-Grady accepted:** provincial autonomy under British trusteeship into the 1950s.
2. **Trusteeship adopted:** statehood postponed.
3. **The Abdullah deal holds:** no Legion invasion; Jerusalem split by agreement.
4. **Bernadotte plan adopted:** Arab Negev, Jewish Galilee, Haifa free port, Jordanian Arab Palestine.
5. **Husni al-Za'im's offer** (Syria, spring 1949, P): peace and resettlement of ~300,000 refugees for half of Lake Tiberias.
6. **Lausanne 1949** (P): Israel's "Gaza plan" and its offer to take back 100,000 refugees.
7. **Altalena becomes an intra-Jewish civil war.**

## 8. Geography (P; approximate)

- **South:** Hebron Arab/Jordan; Isdud and Majdal Arab/Israel; Faluja Arab/Israel; Gaza Arab/Egypt; Beersheba Arab/Israel (Oct 1948); Central Negev Jewish/Israel; al-Auja border/DMZ; Eilat Jewish/Israel (Mar 1949).

## 9. Verified quotations (exact wording only)

1. Declaration of Independence, 14 May 1948 (Avalon, C): "ERETZ-ISRAEL was the birthplace of the Jewish people."; "...HEREBY DECLARE THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A JEWISH STATE IN ERETZ-ISRAEL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE STATE OF ISRAEL."; "...it will ensure complete equality of social and political rights to all its inhabitants irrespective of religion, race or sex"; "...the Arab inhabitants of the State of Israel to preserve peace and participate in the upbuilding of the State"; "WE EXTEND our hand to all neighboring states and their peoples in an offer of peace and good neighborliness".
2. 1939 White Paper (Avalon, C): "it is not part of their policy that Palestine should become a Jewish State."
3. Gromyko, 14 May 1947 (FRUS, C, fragments only): "an independent, dual, democratic, homogeneous Arab-Jewish State"; "the partition of Palestine into two independent autonomous States, one Jewish and one Arab".
4. Resolution 194 para 11 (C): "refugees wishing to return to their homes and live at peace with their neighbours should be permitted to do so".
5. AACI (C): "neither a Jewish state nor an Arab state".
6. Azzam Pasha, *Akhbar al-Yom*, 11 Oct 1947 (C source; meaning disputed, long misdated to May 1948): "I personally wish that the Jews do not drive us to this war, as this will be a war of extermination and momentous massacre". Use only with the 1947 date and context, if at all.

Not verified, do not use: Bevin's "New York" remark; Truman's "hundreds of thousands" remark; any verbatim Magnes, Abdullah or AHC line.

## Terminology

- **The war:** "War of Independence" (Israeli); "al-Nakba" for the exodus and dispossession, "Palestine War" / "Harb 1948" (Palestinian/Arab); neutral "1948 War". Civil-war phase to 14 May, interstate from 15 May.
- **Exodus:** "flight" / "expulsion and flight" (Morris) / "ethnic cleansing" (Pappé, Khalidi).
- **Immigration:** Aliyah Bet / Ha'apala vs "illegal immigration" (British).
- **Jewish underground:** "the revolt" (Begin) vs "terrorism" (British usage, many historians).
- **Deir Yassin:** "massacre" is standard.
- **Operation Agatha** = "Black Saturday".
- **West Bank:** "Judea and Samaria" (Israeli) vs al-Daffa al-Gharbiyya.
- **1949 lines:** the "Green Line", armistice lines, not borders.
- **Jews from Arab lands:** "exodus", "expulsion" or "refugees" vs "aliyah" (contested).
- **Mixed towns:** "liberation" vs "fall" or "occupation".
