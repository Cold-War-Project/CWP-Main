# Research brief: the Suez Question 1946-1957 (companion to cwp_suez_crisis.md)

Research sub-agent, 2026-11-21. Certainty: **C** confirmed in a source the agent fetched or a search result quoting a primary source; **P** probable (agent's knowledge or one weak source, not fetched); **D** disputed (range given). Verdicts on the brief's section 2: **V** verified, **X** corrected, **U** unconfirmed.

Abbreviations: WP-x = English Wikipedia article on x, used as a pointer to its cited scholarship. FRUS = *Foreign Relations of the United States* (1951 vol. V; 1952-54 vol. IX; 1955-57 vols. ## 2. Corrections that change the design

1. **The 1954 re-entry clause does not cover an Israeli attack** (row 7). It covered an outside attack on an Arab League state or Turkey, and lasted 7 years (to Oct 1961). That is why the Sèvres plan needed the "separate the combatants" ultimatum instead. In the brief, *The Canal Base* JE's "re-entry clause" and any collusion path must not let the base holder invoke the clause when the neighbour attacks. Suggested trigger: a hostile great power (or a non-Arab, non-Turkish attacker) attacks the canal holder or an Arab state; it lapses 7 years after the agreement.
2. **Compensation** (row 14): "at market price" was the law's promise; the real outcome was a negotiated lump sum in 1958 under the World Bank, with the company keeping its foreign assets. The *Compensate shareholders* option and the settlement ending can model both: the promise (Bourse price, no money until handover) and the later negotiated deal.
3. **Omega was not a coup plan** (affects 9.8). The OMEGA memorandum (Dulles to Eisenhower, 28 Mar 1956) set out phased pressure: delay Aswan and aid, deny arms licences, back the Baghdad Pact and Iraq's radio, build up King Saud as an alternative Arab leader, and only in phase three covert action in Egypt. No US-funded coup took place. Reports of British (MI6) assassination schemes in 1956 exist (P; S. Dorril, *MI6*, 2000; one-sided and contested). 9.8 can stay as a counterfactual, but its text should say "plan to isolate" and leave "coup" to the game's alternative.
4. **The IMF wasn't vetoed, it was conditional** (row 22). The Sterling bar logic still works; in event text, write "Washington withheld support" rather than "the US blocked the IMF".
5. **Land reform is 1952, not 1953** (row 6). Put it in the first months after the coup.
6. **Garrison size**: use 70,000-80,000, not a flat 80,000.
7. **Bulganin**: the rocket line is a hypothetical question, not an explicit threat. "Threats" stance text should quote it that way (section 5).

## 5. Verified quotations (exact wording as found)
